ALL_MOST_LOCAL

An independent documentary project about places, prices and everyday life, recorded on the ground by a field correspondent.

Chapter One is Chișinău and Moldova: city walks, markets, monasteries and day trips, plus journeys from Chișinău to Romania, Italy and beyond. Every location is checked on site. Prices are noted as they were paid. Music is original.

ALL_MOST_LOCAL Publisher

ALL_MOST_LOCAL Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the ALL_MOST_LOCAL channels. It uploads and schedules the project's own videos on the ALL_MOST_LOCAL YouTube channel after the owner has approved each video, its title and its description. It is not offered to other users.
